# About IntentixLab

IntentixLab is an independent research lab focused on cognitive science and the implications of software architecture for society. We explore how the systems people use shape their behaviour, their interactions with one another, and their participation in social and digital spaces.

## Our philosophy

Our work is guided by a simple principle: software architecture influences human behaviour and social interaction. The choices made in an architecture affect what people can see, understand, control, and consent to, as well as how they relate to others.

We believe those choices should give explicit consideration to privacy, basic mutual decency, and security. These dimensions belong in the design of software and the tools used to build it.

## Our research and tooling

We identify maturity gaps in current technology and explore how appropriate tooling can address them. Our research connects cognitive science with practical questions about how software represents perception, intention, context, and interaction.

Through work such as Intention Space, we investigate architectures and development tools that make software behaviour more explicit, understandable, and open to examination. Our aim is to help developers build systems that support human agency, respectful interaction, and responsible participation in society.
